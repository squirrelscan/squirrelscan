## A

### Accessible name \{#accessible-name\}

The name assistive technology announces for an element, computed from its content, label, aria-label or aria-labelledby.

Also called: aria-label.

Checked by [aria-label: which elements need one and how to check](/rules/a11y/aria-labels), [Accessible names for buttons: what counts and how to check](/rules/a11y/button-name), [Input fields with no accessible name, ARIA and native](/rules/a11y/aria-input-field-name), [Command roles: buttons, links and menu items need names](/rules/a11y/aria-command-name), [Dialog accessible name: role=dialog and the dialog element](/rules/a11y/aria-dialog-name), [Dropdowns with no accessible name: naming a select](/rules/a11y/select-name), [iframe title: naming embedded frames for screen readers](/rules/a11y/frame-title), [Visible label missing from the accessible name](/rules/a11y/label-content-name-mismatch), [Accessible name for a meter element and role=meter](/rules/a11y/aria-meter-name), [Accessible name for a progress bar and role=progressbar](/rules/a11y/aria-progressbar-name), [Checkboxes, radios and switches with no accessible name](/rules/a11y/aria-toggle-field-name), [role=tooltip with no content: what to check](/rules/a11y/aria-tooltip-name), [role=treeitem: naming every node in a tree](/rules/a11y/aria-treeitem-name), [Tables sharing a name, and tables with none](/rules/a11y/table-duplicate-name).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/accessible-name).

### Ad blocker filter lists \{#filter-list\}

Community-maintained rule lists (EasyList, EasyPrivacy) that ad and tracker blockers use to hide elements and block requests.

Also called: EasyList.

Checked by [Adblock cosmetic filters: classes that get hidden](/rules/adblock/element-hiding), [Requests ad blockers stop: links and resources on EasyList](/rules/adblock/blocked-links), [EasyPrivacy matches: analytics that silently does not run](/rules/adblock/privacy-blocked).

### Affiliate disclosure \{#affiliate-disclosure\}

A clear statement that a page earns commission from links, required by the FTC in the US and similar rules elsewhere.

Checked by [Affiliate disclosure: detecting affiliate links without one](/rules/eeat/affiliate-disclosure).

### Agent experience (AX) \{#agent-experience\}

How well a site serves AI agents that read it, find their way around it and act on it, the way UX serves people.

Also called: AX.

Checked by [Content that needs JavaScript: invisible to raw-HTML agents](/rules/ax/content-without-js), [Text to HTML ratio: the token cost of a page for an agent](/rules/ax/token-weight), [Accept: text/markdown: serving Markdown to AI agents](/rules/ax/markdown-response), [AI crawlers in robots.txt: which bots you block, by class](/rules/ax/ai-crawlers), [Blocking AI agents at the edge: what robots.txt hides](/rules/ax/agent-blocking), [llms.txt: what the file is and how squirrel validates it](/rules/ax/llms-txt), [AGENTS.md: the paths squirrel probes and what counts](/rules/ax/agents-md), [MCP and A2A manifests under .well-known: what squirrel finds](/rules/ax/well-known-agent), [Content-Signal in robots.txt: search, ai-input, ai-train](/rules/ax/content-signals), [api-catalog and OpenAPI: how an agent finds your API](/rules/ax/api-discovery).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/agent-experience).

### AGENTS.md \{#agents-md\}

A Markdown file of instructions for coding agents working in a repository or against a docs site.

Checked by [AGENTS.md: the paths squirrel probes and what counts](/rules/ax/agents-md).

### AI crawler \{#ai-crawler\}

A bot that fetches pages for AI companies, in three classes: training crawlers, AI search indexers and user-triggered fetchers, each with its own user-agent token.

Also called: GPTBot, ClaudeBot, Common Crawl.

Checked by [AI crawlers in robots.txt: which bots you block, by class](/rules/ax/ai-crawlers), [Blocking AI agents at the edge: what robots.txt hides](/rules/ax/agent-blocking), [robots.txt: what to put in it and how squirrel checks it](/rules/crawl/robots-txt), [Common Crawl and Wayback: is your site in the archives](/rules/ax/archive-indexing).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/ai-crawler).

### AI-generated content \{#ai-generated-content\}

Text written by a language model; Google's position is that how content is made matters less than whether it is helpful. The related rule is retired and does not run.

Checked by [AI content detection: why this rule is retired](/rules/ai/ai-content).

### Alt text \{#alt-text\}

The alt attribute that gives an image a text alternative for screen readers, failed loads and search engines.

Checked by [Missing alt attribute: what an empty alt means](/rules/images/alt-text), [Redundant alt text: image of, filenames and captions](/rules/a11y/image-redundant-alt), [input type=image without alt: an unnamed submit button](/rules/a11y/input-image-alt), [object elements with no alternative content](/rules/a11y/object-alt).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/alt-text).

### Anchor text \{#anchor-text\}

The visible, clickable text of a link, which tells readers and search engines what the target is about.

Checked by [Empty and generic anchor text: click here and icons](/rules/links/anchor-text), [Link text: why 'click here' fails and what to use](/rules/a11y/link-text), [Identical link text pointing at different pages](/rules/a11y/identical-links-same-purpose).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/anchor-text).

### ARIA \{#aria\}

Accessible Rich Internet Applications: roles, states and properties that describe custom widgets to assistive technology.

Also called: WAI-ARIA.

Checked by [Invalid ARIA role values and how to check them](/rules/a11y/aria-roles), [Invalid aria-* attribute names, aria-labeledby included](/rules/a11y/aria-valid-attr), [Invalid ARIA attribute values and the types they need](/rules/a11y/aria-valid-attr-value), [ARIA attributes that are not allowed on an element](/rules/a11y/aria-allowed-attr), [Required ARIA attributes for a role, and what is missing](/rules/a11y/aria-required-attr), [ARIA roles that require particular child roles](/rules/a11y/aria-required-children), [ARIA roles that require a particular parent role](/rules/a11y/aria-required-parent), [Deprecated and abstract ARIA roles, and what to use instead](/rules/a11y/aria-deprecated-role), [aria-hidden on body or html: what it breaks](/rules/a11y/aria-hidden-body), [Focusable elements inside aria-hidden: the tab trap](/rules/a11y/aria-hidden-focus), [ARIA references pointing at duplicate or missing ids](/rules/a11y/duplicate-id-aria), [role=text with focusable children: why it breaks](/rules/a11y/aria-text).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/aria).

### Article schema \{#article-schema\}

Article, NewsArticle or BlogPosting structured data with headline, dates, author and publisher.

Checked by [Article schema: headline, author and datePublished checks](/rules/schema/article), [An author who is only a name string is not an entity](/rules/schema/entity-authors), [Your pages disagree about who publishes this site](/rules/schema/entity-publisher-mismatch).

### Author byline \{#author-byline\}

The visible author name on an article, ideally linked to an author page and matched by an author entity in structured data.

Checked by [Author bylines on content pages: what share carry one](/rules/eeat/author-byline), [Author expertise: bio pages and Person schema](/rules/eeat/author-expertise), [Author attribution: schema, rel=author and visible bylines](/rules/content/author-info), [An author who is only a name string is not an entity](/rules/schema/entity-authors).

## B

## C

### Canonical URL \{#canonical-url\}

The URL a page declares, with `<link rel="canonical">` or a Link header, as the preferred address for its content.

Also called: rel=canonical.

Checked by [rel=canonical: presence, absolute form and self-reference](/rules/core/canonical), [Link rel=canonical header vs the HTML tag: the mismatch](/rules/core/canonical-header), [Canonicals in two forms: www, scheme and trailing slash](/rules/core/canonical-form-drift), [Canonical URL resolves through a redirect: how to spot it](/rules/crawl/canonical-chain), [Pagination canonicals: why page 2 is not page 1](/rules/crawl/pagination), [og:url versus canonical: why they should be the same URL](/rules/social/og-url-match).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/canonical-url).

### Captions \{#captions\}

Synchronised text of a video's dialogue and sounds, supplied as a `<track kind="captions">` file.

Checked by [Video captions: track elements and embedded players](/rules/a11y/video-captions), [Video captions: the track element and why it is required](/rules/video/video-accessible).

### Character encoding \{#character-encoding\}

The mapping from bytes to characters (normally UTF-8) declared in the Content-Type header or a meta charset tag; a mismatch produces mojibake.

Also called: charset, mojibake.

Checked by [meta charset UTF-8: where it goes and what squirrel checks](/rules/core/charset), [Mojibake: â€™ and other encoding corruption in page text](/rules/content/mojibake).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/character-encoding).

### Client-side redirect \{#client-side-redirect\}

A redirect done in the page (meta refresh or JavaScript) rather than with an HTTP 3xx response.

Also called: meta refresh.

Checked by [meta http-equiv=refresh: redirects and auto-refresh](/rules/a11y/meta-refresh), [Script URL redirects: a 3xx in front of every script load](/rules/perf/js-redirects).

### Client-side rendering \{#client-side-rendering\}

Building page content in the browser with JavaScript; agents and many fetchers that do not run JavaScript see an empty page.

Also called: JavaScript rendering.

Checked by [Content that needs JavaScript: invisible to raw-HTML agents](/rules/ax/content-without-js), [Audits that need a browser: what a static crawl cannot see](/rules/perf/browser-required).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/client-side-rendering).

### Cloaking \{#cloaking\}

Serving search engines or particular user agents different content from what visitors see, a Google spam policy violation.

Also called: hidden text and links.

Checked by [Cloaking: showing Googlebot a different page](/rules/integrity/cloaking), [Hidden text and links: what Google's spam policy covers](/rules/content/hidden-text).

### Color contrast ratio \{#color-contrast\}

The luminance ratio between text and its background; WCAG AA asks for 4.5:1 for body text and 3:1 for large text.

Checked by [Color contrast: the 4.5:1 rule and how to check it](/rules/a11y/color-contrast), [Links in a paragraph told apart by color alone](/rules/a11y/link-in-text-block).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/color-contrast).

### Content freshness \{#content-freshness\}

How current a page is and how consistently it says so: visible dates, dateModified, sitemap lastmod and copyright year.

Checked by [Freshness signals: the dates a page publishes about itself](/rules/content/freshness), [datePublished and dateModified across content pages](/rules/eeat/content-dates), [Visible date disagrees with schema: which date is wrong](/rules/content/date-agreement), [Footer copyright year behind the current year](/rules/content/stale-copyright), [Sitemap lastmod disagrees with the page's dateModified](/rules/crawl/sitemap-lastmod-drift).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/content-freshness).

### Content gap analysis \{#content-gap\}

Finding the topics and queries a site's audience searches for that the site does not yet cover. The related rules are disabled and do not run.

Also called: keyword gap.

Checked by [Content gaps: topic clusters your site does not cover](/rules/gaps/content), [Keyword gaps: terms competitors rank for and you do not](/rules/gaps/keywords).

### Content Security Policy (CSP) \{#content-security-policy\}

A response header listing where a page may load scripts, styles, frames and other resources from, limiting what injected code can do.

Checked by [Content-Security-Policy: what to set and how to check it](/rules/security/csp), [CSP blocks your own scripts, frames and analytics endpoints](/rules/security/csp-blocks-own-resources).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/content-security-policy).

### Content Signals \{#content-signals\}

Content-Signal lines in robots.txt that state whether content may be used for search, AI input or AI training.

Checked by [Content-Signal in robots.txt: search, ai-input, ai-train](/rules/ax/content-signals).

### Cookie attributes \{#cookie-attributes\}

The Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite flags on Set-Cookie that limit where a cookie is sent and whether scripts can read it.

Also called: Secure, HttpOnly, SameSite.

Checked by [Cookie flags: Secure, HttpOnly and SameSite on Set-Cookie](/rules/security/cookie-flags), [Third-party cookies: which external domains a page loads](/rules/security/third-party-cookies).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/cookie-attributes).

### Cookie consent \{#cookie-consent\}

The banner and mechanism that asks visitors before setting non-essential cookies, as GDPR and ePrivacy require in the EU.

Also called: Google Consent Mode.

Checked by [Cookie consent banner: detecting a consent mechanism](/rules/legal/cookie-consent), [Google Consent Mode v2: the four consent parameters](/rules/analytics/consent-mode).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/cookie-consent).

### Core Web Vitals \{#core-web-vitals\}

Google's three user-experience metrics: LCP for loading, INP for responsiveness and CLS for visual stability, judged at the 75th percentile of real visits.

Checked by [LCP image not preloaded: what squirrel counts as a hero](/rules/perf/lcp-hints), [Third-party scripts and INP: what squirrel counts per page](/rules/perf/inp-hints), [Images without width and height: the CLS risk squirrel finds](/rules/perf/cls-hints), [Audits that need a browser: what a static crawl cannot see](/rules/perf/browser-required).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/core-web-vitals).

### Crawl budget \{#crawl-budget\}

The number of URLs a search engine is willing and able to crawl on a site in a given time, set by crawl capacity and crawl demand.

Checked by [Redirect chains: when one hop turns into three](/rules/crawl/redirect-chain), [4XX pages in sitemap: dead URLs you are still submitting](/rules/crawl/sitemap-4xx), [Soft 404: error content served with an HTTP 200 status](/rules/crawl/soft-404), [URL parameters: how many is too many, and what to do](/rules/url/parameters), [Links pointing at redirecting URLs: the hop on every click](/rules/links/redirect-chains).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/crawl-budget).

### Cumulative Layout Shift (CLS) \{#cls\}

A score for how much visible content moves unexpectedly while the page is in use; good is 0.1 or less.

Checked by [Images without width and height: the CLS risk squirrel finds](/rules/perf/cls-hints), [Image width and height attributes: preventing layout shift](/rules/images/dimensions), [font-display swap: the @font-face rule squirrel looks for](/rules/perf/font-loading).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/cls).

## D

### DOM size \{#dom-size\}

The number and depth of elements in a page's document tree; large DOMs slow style calculation and interactions.

Checked by [Large DOM: node count, depth and children per element](/rules/perf/dom-size).

### Doorway page \{#doorway-page\}

A page made to rank for a query and funnel visitors elsewhere, often many near-identical pages per keyword or city.

Checked by [Doorway page: injected affiliate spam on a legitimate site](/rules/integrity/seo-doorway), [Service area pages: the location URLs squirrel looks for](/rules/local/service-area).

### Duplicate content \{#duplicate-content\}

The same or near-identical content reachable at more than one URL, or repeated titles and descriptions across pages.

Checked by [Duplicate title tags: the same title on several pages](/rules/content/duplicate-title), [Duplicate meta descriptions across pages](/rules/content/duplicate-description), [Duplicate page titles: the site-wide uniqueness check](/rules/core/title-unique), [rel=canonical: presence, absolute form and self-reference](/rules/core/canonical), [Trailing slash: /page and /page/ are two different URLs](/rules/url/trailing-slash), [Uppercase in a URL: the duplicate content it creates](/rules/url/lowercase), [URL parameters: how many is too many, and what to do](/rules/url/parameters).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/duplicate-content).

## F

### FAQ schema \{#faq-schema\}

FAQPage structured data marking up question-and-answer pairs; Google now shows FAQ rich results only for a narrow set of sites.

Also called: FAQPage.

Checked by [FAQPage schema: mainEntity, Question and acceptedAnswer](/rules/schema/faq).

### fetchpriority \{#fetchpriority\}

An HTML attribute (high, low, auto) that hints how urgently the browser should fetch an image, script or link.

Checked by [fetchpriority='high': the LCP image hint squirrel checks](/rules/perf/lcp-fetchpriority).

### font-display \{#font-display\}

The @font-face descriptor (swap, optional, fallback) that decides what text shows while a web font loads.

Also called: web font loading.

Checked by [font-display swap: the @font-face rule squirrel looks for](/rules/perf/font-loading), [Google Fonts blocking render: the font CSS squirrel flags](/rules/perf/font-delivery).

### Form labels \{#form-labels\}

The `<label>` or accessible name tied to each form control, plus autocomplete tokens, so people know what to enter.

Checked by [Form labels: which inputs need one and how to check](/rules/a11y/form-labels), [One control, two labels: why multiple labels confuse](/rules/a11y/form-field-multiple-labels), [autocomplete: the right token for every form field](/rules/a11y/autocomplete-tokens), [Input type, enterkeyhint and novalidate on forms](/rules/a11y/input-types), [Inputs that block paste, and why that fails users](/rules/a11y/paste-inputs).

### Fragment link \{#anchor-link\}

A link to an id within a page (#section); it is broken when no element on the target page carries that id.

Also called: jump link, #anchor.

Checked by [Broken anchor links: #fragments that point at nothing](/rules/links/broken-anchors).

## G

### Generative engine optimization (GEO) \{#generative-engine-optimization\}

Making content more likely to be retrieved, cited and quoted by AI answer engines; the term comes from a 2023 research paper.

Also called: GEO, AEO.

Checked by [AI crawlers in robots.txt: which bots you block, by class](/rules/ax/ai-crawlers), [Content that needs JavaScript: invisible to raw-HTML agents](/rules/ax/content-without-js), [llms.txt: what the file is and how squirrel validates it](/rules/ax/llms-txt), [LLM parsability score: what it measures and how to raise it](/rules/ai/llm-parsability), [Invalid JSON-LD syntax: what breaks it and how to fix](/rules/schema/json-ld-valid), [Blocking AI agents at the edge: what robots.txt hides](/rules/ax/agent-blocking).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/generative-engine-optimization).

### Geo meta tags \{#geo-meta-tags\}

Legacy geo.region, geo.placename and geo.position meta tags that state a page's location; not to be confused with GEO, generative engine optimization.

Also called: geo.position, ICBM.

Checked by [geo.placename: what geo meta tags do and who reads them](/rules/local/geo-meta).

[Read the full definition](/glossary/geo-meta-tags).

### Googlebot file size limit \{#googlebot-file-size-limit\}

The byte limit after which Googlebot stops reading a fetched HTML file or PDF; content past it is not indexed.

Checked by [HTML document size: when a page is too big to crawl fully](/rules/crawl/html-size), [PDF size: large linked documents and what gets indexed](/rules/crawl/pdf-size).

### GraphQL introspection \{#graphql-introspection\}

The GraphQL feature that returns the full schema on request; left on in production it maps the API for attackers.

Checked by [GraphQL introspection in production: what an open schema exposes](/rules/security/graphql-introspection), [GraphQL mutations over GET: why they open the door to CSRF](/rules/security/graphql-get-mutations).

### HTTPS \{#https\}

## I

### @id (JSON-LD node identifier) \{#entity-id\}

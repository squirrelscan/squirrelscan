title: "Structured Data"
description: "Structured data and rich snippet eligibility"
---

Structured Data rules read the JSON-LD a page carries and check it against what each schema.org type needs. Most run per page and name the exact property that is missing; two run across the whole crawl, one comparing coverage against the site's own norm and one checking that a rating is visible and about the page it sits on. Markup that will not parse is reported before anything else, since nothing downstream can read it.

See [Fix SEO issues with AI](https://squirrelscan.com/learn/fix-seo-issues-with-ai) for how an agent works through a report.

## Rules

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Article schema: headline, author and datePublished checks" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/article">
    Article JSON-LD missing headline, author or datePublished, or carrying a date that is not ISO 8601. squirrel checks both on every page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="BreadcrumbList schema: positions, names and what breaks" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/breadcrumb">
    Breadcrumb markup with no itemListElement, items missing position or name, or positions out of order. squirrel checks every non-homepage URL.
  </Card>
  <Card title="FAQPage schema: mainEntity, Question and acceptedAnswer" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/faq">
    FAQ markup with no mainEntity, or Question entries missing name or acceptedAnswer. squirrel checks the structure on every page that carries it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Invalid JSON-LD syntax: what breaks it and how to fix" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/json-ld-valid">
    A structured data block that will not parse, or parses but omits properties schema.org requires. squirrel names the exact type and property.
  </Card>
  <Card title="LocalBusiness schema: required name, address or areaServed" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/local-business">
    LocalBusiness markup with no name, an incomplete PostalAddress, or neither an address nor a service area. squirrel checks all three.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Organization schema: name, url, logo and sameAs checks" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/organization">
    Organization JSON-LD missing name or url, with no logo, or a sameAs list that omits a social profile the site clearly runs. squirrel finds all three.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product schema: name, image, offers and review checks" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/product">
    Product markup with no name or image, an Offer missing price or availability, and no rating data. squirrel checks each product page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AggregateRating on the wrong page: visible and on-topic" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/rating-scope">
    A sitewide star rating stamped on privacy, terms and blog pages, or one no reader can see. squirrel flags both as policy risks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AggregateRating: ratingValue, ratingCount and review checks" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/review">
    Rating markup missing ratingValue or a review count, and Review nodes with no author or reviewRating. squirrel checks both shapes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Structured data coverage: pages that break your own norm" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/coverage-outlier">
    Forty of forty-five product pages carry Product markup and five do not. squirrel finds the gap against the site's own pattern, not a generic rule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="VideoObject schema: required props and duration format" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/video">
    A page with an embedded video and no VideoObject markup, or markup missing thumbnailUrl, uploadDate or an ISO 8601 duration. squirrel checks both.
  </Card>
  <Card title="WebSite SearchAction and the retired sitelinks searchbox" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/website-search">
    WebSite schema on a homepage with no SearchAction, or a SearchAction missing target or query-input. squirrel checks the homepage markup.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Disable all Structured Data rules

```toml squirrel.toml
[rules]
disable = ["schema/*"]
```

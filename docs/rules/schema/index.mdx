Structured Data rules work at two scales. Per-page rules read the JSON-LD a page carries and name the exact property each schema.org type is missing; markup that will not parse is reported before anything else, since nothing downstream can read it.

The rest read the [entity map](/entity-map), the whole site's JSON-LD collapsed into one graph. That is where the expensive failures live: sixty valid `Organization` blocks with nothing to say they are one organization pass every per-page check and still leave a search engine with sixty companies. Thirteen rules cover identity, agreement and reachability across the crawl, and [fixing structured data identity](/entity-map/fixing) has the change to make in each generator.

See [Fix SEO issues with AI](https://squirrelscan.com/learn/fix-seo-issues-with-ai) for how an agent works through a report.

## Rules

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Article schema: headline, author and datePublished checks" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/article">
    Article JSON-LD missing headline, author or datePublished, or carrying a date that is not ISO 8601. squirrel checks both on every page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="BreadcrumbList schema: positions, names and what breaks" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/breadcrumb">
    Breadcrumb markup with no itemListElement, items missing position or name, or positions out of order. squirrel checks every non-homepage URL.
  </Card>
  <Card title="FAQPage schema: mainEntity, Question and acceptedAnswer" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/faq">
    FAQ markup with no mainEntity, or Question entries missing name or acceptedAnswer. squirrel checks the structure on every page that carries it.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Invalid JSON-LD syntax: what breaks it and how to fix" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/json-ld-valid">
    A structured data block that will not parse, or parses but omits properties schema.org requires. squirrel names the exact type and property.
  </Card>
  <Card title="LocalBusiness schema: required name, address or areaServed" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/local-business">
    LocalBusiness markup with no name, an incomplete PostalAddress, or neither an address nor a service area. squirrel checks all three.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Organization schema: name, url, logo and sameAs checks" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/organization">
    Organization JSON-LD missing name or url, with no logo, or a sameAs list that omits a social profile the site clearly runs. squirrel finds all three.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Product schema: name, image, offers and review checks" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/product">
    Product markup with no name or image, an Offer missing price or availability, and no rating data. squirrel checks each product page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AggregateRating on the wrong page: visible and on-topic" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/rating-scope">
    A sitewide star rating stamped on privacy, terms and blog pages, or one no reader can see. squirrel flags both as policy risks.
  </Card>
  <Card title="AggregateRating: ratingValue, ratingCount and review checks" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/review">
    Rating markup missing ratingValue or a review count, and Review nodes with no author or reviewRating. squirrel checks both shapes.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Structured data coverage: pages that break your own norm" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/coverage-outlier">
    Forty of forty-five product pages carry Product markup and five do not. squirrel finds the gap against the site's own pattern, not a generic rule.
  </Card>
  <Card title="VideoObject schema: required props and duration format" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/video">
    A page with an embedded video and no VideoObject markup, or markup missing thumbnailUrl, uploadDate or an ISO 8601 duration. squirrel checks both.
  </Card>
  <Card title="WebSite SearchAction and the retired sitelinks searchbox" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/website-search">
    WebSite schema on a homepage with no SearchAction, or a SearchAction missing target or query-input. squirrel checks the homepage markup.
  </Card>
  <Card title="One organization, or sixty? Entities declared without an @id" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/entity-identity">
    The same organization on every page with nothing to say they are one thing. squirrel reads the whole crawl, not one page.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Two plugins, two @ids, one organization split in half" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/entity-split-identity">
    Yoast declares your organization under one @id and WordLift under another. A search engine reads two companies.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A publisher that points at nothing" icon="triangle-exclamation" href="/rules/schema/entity-dangling">
    Every article references an organization by @id and only the homepage declares it. squirrel checks against the whole crawl.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The same entity, two different logos" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-conflicts">
    One organization declared on forty pages with two logos or two phone numbers. A search engine picks one.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A bare #organization is one entity per page, not one entity" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-id-format">
    A relative @id resolves against the page it sits on, so the same fragment on forty pages is forty identifiers.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An Organization on one page, a LocalBusiness on another" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-type-drift">
    The same @id with different @type sets. Types drive eligibility, and which set wins is not specified.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An author who is only a name string is not an entity" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-authors">
    Articles with a byline and no Person entity. Most of what E-E-A-T is built on needs an identifier.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your pages disagree about who publishes this site" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-publisher-mismatch">
    Two hundred articles name one publisher and three name another. squirrel weighs them by how much of the site says each.
  </Card>
  <Card title="The whole LocalBusiness block, on every single page" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-local-business-per-page">
    The default of most page builders, and why so many local sites disagree with themselves about their own phone number.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Nothing says these pages are one site" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-website-missing">
    A WebSite entity carries your site name in search results and is what every page should point back to with isPartOf.
  </Card>
  <Card title="A business with nothing to attach its reputation to" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-organization-missing">
    This site has the pages a business has and no Organization entity for a search engine to hang any of it on.
  </Card>
  <Card title="Your organization is not connected to anything that corroborates it" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-sameas-missing">
    sameAs is how a search engine learns the company on your site is the company on LinkedIn and Wikidata.
  </Card>
  <Card title="An @id that nothing points at" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/schema/entity-orphan">
    Giving an entity an identifier is the expensive part. One that nothing references is doing no more than an inline block.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

## Disable all Structured Data rules

```toml squirrel.toml
[rules]
disable = ["schema/*"]
```

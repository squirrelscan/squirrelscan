## What content recommendations are

[Content gaps](/rules/gaps/content) compare you against search demand and competitors. Content recommendations are generative: squirrel sends a slim inventory of the pages it crawled (URL, title, word count, last-modified date and crawl depth) to the cloud service, which writes prioritised suggestions with an LLM. No competitor data is needed.

Each suggestion has a title, a written rationale and a priority of high, medium or low. The suggestions fall into five categories:

- **Net-new topics**: content the site should own given its niche and audience.
- **Content refresh**: existing pages that are thin or stale. Each one names the page and the signal that triggered it, a low word count or an old modified date.
- **Cluster completion**: topic clusters the site started but did not finish, and orphan or deeply buried pages that need linking in.
- **Formats**: FAQ, comparison, how-to, glossary or landing pages where the niche warrants them.
- **Funnel coverage**: top, middle or bottom of funnel stages with no content.

## What squirrel checks

The rule is site-wide and reads a result the cloud content-recommendations service produced before the rules ran.

- `content-recommendations` is skipped when no result is available, because the rule was not enabled, you are on the free plan, the audit is local-only, or the service skipped. Message: `Content recommendations skipped`, with a readable skip reason.
- `content-recommendations` passes when the service had no suggestions. Message: `No content recommendations`.
- One info check per category that has suggestions: `recommendations-net-new-topics`, `recommendations-content-refresh`, `recommendations-cluster-completion`, `recommendations-formats` and `recommendations-funnel-coverage`. Message: `3 net-new topic recommendations`, or `1 content refresh recommendation` for one. Items are listed high priority first.

Severity for the rule is info and the weight is 1. The suggestions appear in the console, `llm`, JSON and HTML reports with the rest of the Gap Analysis section.

## How to fix it

Start with the high-priority items. Refresh suggestions are the cheapest win: the page already exists and is already indexed, so expanding or updating it is faster than writing from scratch. Treat net-new topics as a content plan, and link each new page from the pages in its cluster.

```toml squirrel.toml
[rule_options."gaps/recommendations"]
country = "AU"

```

## Enable / disable

This rule is opt-in - enable it explicitly:

```toml squirrel.toml
[rules]
enable = ["*", "gaps/recommendations"]
```

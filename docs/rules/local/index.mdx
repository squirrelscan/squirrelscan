---

Local SEO rules cover the signals a business with a physical location or a service area publishes: LocalBusiness schema, one consistent phone number and postal address across the site, location pages and geo meta tags. Every rule in this category is skipped, with the reason shown in the report, when the site profile says the site is not a local business, so a global product site is never marked down for them.

See [Fix SEO issues with AI](https://squirrelscan.com/learn/fix-seo-issues-with-ai) for how an agent works through a report.

  <Card title="Service area pages: the location URLs squirrel looks for" icon="circle-info" href="/rules/local/service-area">

</CardGroup>

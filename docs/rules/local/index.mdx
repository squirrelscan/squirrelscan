Local SEO rules cover the signals a business with a physical location or a service area publishes: LocalBusiness schema, one consistent phone number and postal address across the site, location pages and geo meta tags. Every rule in this category is skipped, with the reason shown in the report, when the site profile says the site is not a local business, so a global product site is never marked down for them.

<Note>Geo meta tags are not GEO. [Geo meta tags](/glossary#geo-meta-tags) such as `geo.placename` and `geo.position` name the place a page is about, and Google says it ignores them ([local/geo-meta](/rules/local/geo-meta) has the details). [GEO, generative engine optimization](/glossary#generative-engine-optimization), is about being retrieved and cited by AI answer engines. squirrelscan does not measure GEO; the rules that bear on it, such as AI crawler access and llms.txt, are in [Agent experience](/rules/ax).</Note>

## Run only these rules

```bash
squirrel audit https://example.com --rule-include local
```

## Where to start

These carry the highest severity and weight in the category.

- [NAP consistency: same name, address and phone everywhere](/rules/local/nap-consistency): One phone number or address written several ways across a site. squirrel finds the site norm and lists every page that disagrees with it.
- [Service area pages: the location URLs squirrel looks for](/rules/local/service-area): A local business with no /locations or /service-areas page. squirrel scans the crawled paths and reports which location hubs it found.
- [geo.placename: what geo meta tags do and who reads them](/rules/local/geo-meta): Location pages carrying no geo.region, geo.placename, geo.position or ICBM tag. squirrel reports which of the four are present, page by page.

## Key terms

- [Doorway page](/glossary#doorway-page): A page made to rank for a query and funnel visitors elsewhere, often many near-identical pages per keyword or city.
- [Geo meta tags](/glossary#geo-meta-tags): Legacy geo.region, geo.placename and geo.position meta tags that state a page's location; not to be confused with GEO, generative engine optimization.
- [LocalBusiness schema](/glossary#local-business-schema): LocalBusiness structured data with address, opening hours and geo coordinates for a physical business.
- [NAP consistency](/glossary#nap-consistency): Name, address and phone number stated the same way across a site's pages, schema and listings.

- New rule `links/generic-anchor-density`: measures generic anchor text ("click here", "read more", "learn more", "here", "this page") as a site-wide share of internal content anchors, not a per-page note. It warns at 20% and fails at 40% by default (`warnRatio`, `failRatio`), lists the ten pages with the most generic anchors, and skips a crawl with fewer than 30 anchors (`minAnchors`). Nav, header, footer and aside links are not counted. The generic phrase list is now shared with `links/anchor-text` and `a11y/link-text`, which both gain `this page`. See [links/generic-anchor-density](https://docs.squirrelscan.com/rules/links/generic-anchor-density). (squirrelscan/repo#1387)

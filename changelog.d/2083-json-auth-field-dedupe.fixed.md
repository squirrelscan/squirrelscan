- A password, password hash or secret field that both `security/json-sensitive-fields` and `security/auth-endpoint-disclosure` find on a current-user or session endpoint such as `/api/account` is now reported once, by `security/auth-endpoint-disclosure`, and the endpoint is requested once per audit. Each rule reports on its own, and after the site rules finish squirrel drops only the overlapping field from `security/json-sensitive-fields`, which keeps its other fields, such as card numbers and access tokens.

- New rule `integrity/chrome-outlier` flags pages left on an outdated or broken site template: pages in small template clusters and singletons on a site where one template covers at least 70% of pages (10 pages minimum) that lack that template's navigation, its footer, all of its stylesheets or all of its CSS and script hosts. A page that only adds a route-level stylesheet or a body class, or keeps the site stylesheet while lacking a post stylesheet, an image CDN or a preconnect host, is not reported. It reads the stored template fingerprint, skips pages that `integrity/template-discontinuity` already reports, and warns on a few stragglers or fails when they reach 10% of the crawl. See [integrity/chrome-outlier](https://docs.squirrelscan.com/rules/integrity/chrome-outlier). (squirrelscan/repo#1367)

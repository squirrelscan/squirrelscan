- `squirrel audit --render-cdp <url>` (and `[render] cdp_url` in `squirrel.toml`) renders pages in a Chrome you run, over the DevTools Protocol. It works signed out, sends nothing to the squirrelscan cloud for rendering, reaches staging hosts and pages behind a login, and takes `--render-mode auto|all`. A page the browser cannot render, or an unreachable endpoint, falls back to plain HTTP and the report says why (#2306)

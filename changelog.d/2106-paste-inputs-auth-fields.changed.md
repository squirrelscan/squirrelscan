- `a11y/paste-inputs` now reports paste blocking on password and one-time-code fields only (`type="password"`, or `autocomplete` of `current-password`, `new-password` or `one-time-code`) and maps to WCAG 2.2 success criterion 3.3.8 Accessible Authentication (Minimum), so `--wcag-level AA` keeps it. Paste, copy or cut blocking on other fields, and copy or cut blocking on password fields, is no longer reported. (#2106)

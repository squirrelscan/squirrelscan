- `security/leaked-secrets` findings carry `meta.context`: a redacted snippet of up to 120 characters either side of the match, so you can find the key in the source without refetching it. The key is masked as in the item id. Nearby quoted literals keep none of their characters, whatever their length, and a stray apostrophe (as in a `// don't` comment) does not unmask an assigned value, argument or list item after it. Unquoted assigned values, auth-scheme tokens, long token-shaped runs, letter-and-digit runs and URL credentials are replaced by asterisks too. See [security/leaked-secrets](https://docs.squirrelscan.com/rules/security/leaked-secrets). (squirrelscan/repo#2215)

- **New rule `content/near-duplicate`.** Groups indexable pages whose main text is nearly identical: the same page with a city name, a date or a sentence swapped, a small edit (location pages, programmatic landing pages), which `content/duplicate-content` cannot see because it only matches exact copies. Each page's main text (navigation, header, footer, sidebars and forms dropped) is reduced to a 64-bit simhash over distinct word triples (a repeated phrase counts once), and pages within 5 bits of each other form a cluster that lists every member URL. A paragraph rewritten out of several is beyond the limit and is not reported. A cluster of 10 or more distinct texts fails (doorway scale), a smaller one warns. Exact copies inside a cluster are counted and left to `content/duplicate-content`, a group made only of exact copies is not reported twice, and pages that share one canonical or are hreflang versions of each other are left out. Skips a crawl under 10 pages. The hash is stored per page (`page_features.content_simhash`, schema version 46) so the streaming and legacy paths give the same answer. (squirrelscan/repo#1369)

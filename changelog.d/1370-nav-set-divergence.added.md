- New rule `links/nav-set-divergence`: flags pages whose navigation is missing links the rest of the site has: a landing page built outside the shared layout, an older template, a header or footer include that failed to render. The norm is every internal header, navigation and footer link carried by at least 70% of the pages, and it must be shared by at least 70% of them to count. A page missing norm links is reported with the exact hrefs (a warning, or a failure when it is missing at least half the norm). A section with its own navigation, such as a docs sidebar, is compared within its page type and is not flagged against the marketing nav, and a localized section (`/fr/`, `/de/`) is compared within its own locale. The rule skips a crawl under 10 pages, under 10 pages with navigation links, or with no clear norm. It is a normal local rule: no model, no account, no surcharge. The per-page set is stored in `page_features.nav_link_set` (schema version 45) so the streaming and legacy paths give the same answer. See [links/nav-set-divergence](https://docs.squirrelscan.com/rules/links/nav-set-divergence). (squirrelscan/repo#1370)

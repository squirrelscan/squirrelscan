- A leaking current-user endpoint such as `/api/me` is now always reported exactly once. `security/unauthenticated-api` reports every endpoint it finds, and squirrel keeps one finding per endpoint after the site rules finish: the `security/auth-endpoint-disclosure` finding when that rule reported the endpoint, otherwise the `security/unauthenticated-api` one. Before, an endpoint handed to `security/auth-endpoint-disclosure` lost its finding when that rule threw an error or ran past its time budget. (#2082) For JSON consumers: the `deferredToAuth` detail on the `security/unauthenticated-api` clean row is replaced by `reportedBy` and `reportedElsewhere`.
